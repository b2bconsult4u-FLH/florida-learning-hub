Today in Florida History · September 11

Two Florida Stories of National Significance

2001 — President Bush Learns of the September 11 Attacks in Sarasota. The president was visiting Emma E. Booker Elementary School when he received word that the United States was under attack and delivered his first public response from the school.

Read: September 11 in Sarasota

1964 — The Beatles Play an Integrated Jacksonville Concert. After Hurricane Dora diverted them to Key West, the Beatles reached Jacksonville and performed at the Gator Bowl before an integrated audience.
