Today in Florida History · November 15, 1905

Tampa Opens the Florida State Fair

By Wm. E. McMullen II

On Wednesday, November 15, 1905, Tampa opened a state fair that gave visitors a reason to look in two directions. Inside the grounds were agricultural exhibitions. In the streets was an opening-day parade. The official program called for Ye Mystic Krewe of Gasparilla to lead a flower parade beginning at the Tampa Bay gates, passing through principal streets and returning to the fairgrounds. The route made the city part of the show. [1] [2]

The souvenir program tells a second story through its advertisements. A bottling company offered soft drinks. A laundry promised to care for customers’ linen. A real estate seller advertised an office. Those pages put tradespeople and merchants alongside the exhibitors. Someone who picked up the program to find the parade could also come home with the name of a business to visit. The fair gave advertisers access to readers who had a reason to keep the booklet. [1]

The organizers also printed information about the association conducting the fair and the state’s appropriation for prizes. An agricultural prize offered public recognition, and the prizes gave exhibitors a reason to bring their work before people outside their own communities. Gasparilla helped give the event a Tampa identity. A later Florida Historical Society account traces how the fair and carnival remained linked in Tampa, though the names used by organizers changed over time. [1] [3]

Read the program as a plan for the event. It tells us what organizers advertised, where they expected people to go, and which businesses wanted their attention. A newspaper published afterward could answer a different question: what actually happened when the crowds arrived? The opening date is clear in the program. The archive’s short catalog description and the printed cover give different closing dates, so the opening is the focus here. [1] [2]

Classroom connection

Pick an advertisement and one scheduled activity. What does each reveal about the people organizers expected to attend?
