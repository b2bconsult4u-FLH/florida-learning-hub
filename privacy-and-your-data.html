Privacy & Your Data

A plain-language explanation of what Florida Learning Hub does and does not collect

Florida Learning Hub does not directly collect names, email addresses, student information, account information, or other personal information from visitors. The website uses Cloudflare’s cookie-free analytics and security services to measure aggregate website traffic, page visits, performance, and security activity.

Cloudflare hosts and protects Florida Learning Hub. This information helps us understand which pages are being visited, confirm that the website is loading properly, and identify bots, attacks, or unusual traffic. Florida Learning Hub does not use this information to identify individual visitors, create personal profiles, or track people across other websites.

Statement adopted: September 2026.
